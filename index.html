Everything, open and shipped

Every planned feature and known bug of SkyrimCoop, the Skyrim co-op mod, and how far along each one is. Nothing is released yet and nothing is playable: the mod has been tested in a game only as far as two games finding each other through Steam. Items marked “to decide” or “idea” are not promises. Each item goes through eight steps from queued to shipped; the yellow step is where it is now.
